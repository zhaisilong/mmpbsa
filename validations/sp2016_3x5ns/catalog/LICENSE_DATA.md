# Data licensing and provenance

Files under `sources/mmcif` are unmodified RCSB/wwPDB downloads covered by the
wwPDB CC0 1.0 data-use policy.  Preserve accession, revision date, retrieval
date, byte length, and SHA-256 when deriving another validation.

Benchmark labels are attributed to Spiliotopoulos et al., *Frontiers in
Molecular Biosciences* 3:46 (2016), DOI 10.3389/fmolb.2016.00046, published
under CC BY 4.0.  No old code, trajectory, topology, log, or generated MMPBSA
result is copied here.  See `licenses.json` and `provenance.json`.
